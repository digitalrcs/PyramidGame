How to play

A text adventure rewards attention. Read the room, examine its objects, take notes and try the actions suggested by its inscriptions.

Your first steps

  1. Sign in or create an explorer account. Confirm your emailed verification link before entering the game, or ask an administrator to approve your address. Your solo journey then begins at the desert threshold.
  2. Type GET LAMP, LIGHT LAMP and GET BOTTLE.
  3. Move with N, S, E, W or GO EAST. LOOK repeats the room.
  4. Use EXAMINE and READ to study clues. GET takes an object; DROP leaves it. Your satchel holds eight objects.
  5. Recover three relics on each pyramid level and DEPOSIT at the landing. Record the study tablet, solve the gate riddle and ASCEND.

Your account and email

Choose an explorer name and provide your email when registering. Confirm the emailed verification link before playing. Until approval, signing in opens only the verification and account screen, where you can resend the link or correct your address. Administrator approval also unlocks play. Password recovery and invitation email require an approved address. Existing explorers can still sign in by username and add email in Account. Use Forgot password? to request a reset link. Account also controls optional invitation and information emails. Email addresses are not shown in the explorers list.

Start alone, invite later

Open Expeditions. Create a named world and choose an adventure. Inviting someone is optional: you can begin immediately. Select an online explorer from the dropdown or type the name of an existing account, even if they are offline.

Your companion selects your expedition from their dropdown and accepts the invitation whenever ready. Open games allow join requests; the host approves them first. Both players keep separate positions, satchels and journals while sharing doors, puzzles and floor objects.

Help each other

Meet in the same room and use:

SHARE ITEM rope WITH Mrs_Player
GET rope FROM Wayfarer
SHARE INFORMATION The eastern wall has a bronze ring.
SHARE JOURNAL WITH Mrs_Player
OFFERS

Only awake players in the same room can exchange items or information. Items require an offer and acceptance. Sign-out leaves you in a trance with your possessions saved; closing a tab takes up to 45 seconds to show as a trance. In the original pyramid, awake companions gather at the gate to ascend together; sleeping companions travel with the party.

Your map, notes and terminal

  • MAP opens a gold schematic of rooms you personally visited in the current realm. It costs 2 points every time. Unknown rooms and exits to them are omitted. Closing the map is free.
  • CLEAR clears your saved transcript only. Your possessions, progress, journal and companions’ transcripts remain.
  • NOTE your observation writes to your journal. JOURNAL or the Journal button reads it.
  • HINT costs 5 points. HELP lists commands. RETREAT returns to the landing.
  • A soft chime on entering a lit room means there are loose items or an unfinished mechanism to investigate. It does not reveal the solution. Use CHIMES ON/OFF to mute it. Browser sound starts after interacting with the page.

Playing on a phone

Use Play, Map, Satchel & journal, Explorers and Adventures to move between panels. Menu contains your account, guides and sign-out. The same commands, saves and expeditions work on desktop and mobile.

Mobile maps start with readable room-and-route cards and put your current room first. Choose Drawing to swipe through the gold schematic. Switching views is free; reopening the map costs another two points. Both views hide rooms you have not visited.

Earn extra points

  • +1 for each newly discovered room beyond the starting room.
  • +10 for visiting every room in a level or realm, once per realm.
  • +5 to the giver when a companion accepts a distinct item: up to three rewarded items per level/realm. Lamps do not earn a sharing bonus.
  • +3 for the first journal share that actually adds new information to a companion’s journal, once per expedition.

Repeated visits and passing the same item back and forth do not repeatedly reward the same giver. Map and hint costs persist even when the displayed score is zero; later points first offset those costs.

Build your own adventures

Administrators can install reviewed JSON adventures and portal expansions. Read the Player, API and Mod guide for the complete command reference, HTTP endpoints, content fields, examples and installation rules.

Choose an adventure

After signing in, choose a saved journey with RESUME, or select NEW EXPEDITION on an adventure card, name it and choose BEGIN ADVENTURE. You can play alone without inviting anyone. Each expedition saves separately. Resume a saved world from the same panel; RETURN TO ORIGINAL SOLO SAVE brings you back to your original Pyramid journey.

Design an adventure

Choose CREATE AN ADVENTURE from the adventure library to build a JSON content pack. Drafts stay in this browser. Export a copy to keep or share it; an administrator reviews and installs packs before they appear for everyone.

Rooms that change

LOOK shows current loose items and solved mechanisms. Earlier transcript entries remain a record of what you saw then. READ a scroll or other readable item to copy its writing into your journal once.

Maps separate vertical passages into relative floors and label one-way holes, stairs, ladders, bridges and portals. Only visited rooms appear. Sound cues mark points, puzzles, arrivals and visible changes; CHIMES OFF mutes them.

In the designer, use Changing room descriptions to author before/after states and EXAMINE clues. Preview conditions before exporting. Readables adds writing to items; passage types label the map.
