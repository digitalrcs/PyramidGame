# Shared expeditions

Open EXPEDITIONS and create a named world. Choose Chamber of the Gods or
an installed content pack. A private world is invitation-only. Opening
join requests makes it visible to other signed-in explorers, but the host
must approve each request. The invited explorer then accepts. Hosts invite
existing accounts by exact explorer name. Up to eight explorers can join;
each account may host up to ten saved worlds.

Everyone has a separate location, satchel, journal and command transcript.
Doors, floor objects, recovered relics, mechanisms and final arrangement
belong to the shared world. Each entrant receives a personal lit lamp.
One person can go east while another goes west. Players in the same room
see arrivals and LOOK reports companions. The companion list updates every
five seconds while the browser tab is visible; it is not instant chat.

```text
SHARE ITEM bronze key WITH ExplorerTwo
GET bronze key FROM ExplorerOne
OFFERS
SHARE INFORMATION The inscription mentions the rising sun.
SHARE JOURNAL WITH ExplorerTwo
```

Use full item and explorer names. An item stays in its owner's satchel
until accepted, and both players must be awake in the same room. You cannot
take another person's possessions without an offer. Moving, switching
worlds or signing out cancels your outstanding offers. Information reaches
only awake companions in the room. Journal sharing copies up to the
recipient's 100-note limit; private notes are shared too, so review first.
Messages are plain text. The last 180 transcript entries are retained.

Closing the tab leaves your explorer in a trance after 45 seconds;
signing out or switching worlds does so immediately. Location and satchel
persist. Resume that expedition to wake in the saved room. Important tools
held by a sleeping player remain theirs: offer or drop shared tools before
leaving for a long time. Players cannot take an unoffered item. Account administrators can end sessions or suspend access.
In Chamber of the Gods, awake players gather at the gate to ASCEND;
sleeping companions travel to the next landing with the group. This is the
one intentional exception to resuming at the exact room where you left.
Mod realms allow players to pass through portals independently.

If two actions compete, one is rejected with a refreshed state. Review the
new situation and retry; commands are never silently replayed. EXPEDITIONS
lets you return to your original solo save or resume another shared world.

## Starting without your companions

Use the expedition dropdown to select a saved, invited or open world. When creating an expedition, choose an online explorer or type the name of an existing offline account. Inviting someone is optional: the host can begin immediately, and a companion can accept later. An invitation does not create an account.

Your solo save remains available through the expedition selector. See the [player guide](Player-Guide) for commands and rewards and [accounts and email](Accounts-and-Email) for notification behavior.

## Shared changes and private discoveries

A companion can open a route or pick up an object while you remain in the room. The live object list and authored conditional descriptions use the shared world state; LOOK gives the latest scene. Arrivals and visible changes can produce a short notice and sound. Repeated refreshes do not keep replaying an unchanged event. CHIMES OFF mutes all game cues in your browser without affecting anyone else.

Maps and journals remain personal. A companion exploring upstairs does not reveal those rooms on your map. Reading a portable item adds its writing only to your journal; use SHARE JOURNAL when you want to pass the clues on. Objects held by another explorer are not readable from your own inventory or room view. Offer and accept the object, or share the information in person.
